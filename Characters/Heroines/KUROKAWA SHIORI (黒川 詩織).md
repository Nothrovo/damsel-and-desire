## Personality
Quiet, hyper-observant, and socially awkward, Shiori spends most of her days hiding behind paperbacks and oversized sleeves to keep the world at an arm's length. Inside, however, her emotional world is an intense, volatile tempest. Prone to severe mood swings and crippling fear of abandonment, she swings between self-loathing isolation and fierce, suffocating devotion. 

When she feels alienated, she retreats into dark poetry and biting inner monologues; but once someone breaks through her shell and shows her genuine kindness, the floodgates open completely. She doesn't just fall in love—she latches on with every fiber of her being. Her devotion is absolute, borderline obsessive, and fiercely loyal; she keeps track of every tiny habit of the person she cares about, anticipates their needs before they speak, and would willingly burn the world down (or stay up three nights straight handwriting cheat sheets) just to make their life a fraction easier.

## Backstory

### "Asleep"
Shiori grew up in a house built entirely out of stiff silence and polite coldness. Her father was an overworked corporate salaryman who only cared about academic perfection, while her mother was a perfectionist classical piano teacher whose praise was conditioned strictly on pristine results. Mistakes weren't met with shouting; they were met with days of suffocating silent treatment, leaving young Shiori perpetually guessing what part of her existence was wrong. 

In middle school, the pressure cracked her wide open—she stopped fitting in, her grades slipped, and the resulting alienation at school made her a ghost in her own classroom. Whenever the screaming in her own head got unbearable, she escaped to indie bands and gloomy classic literature, hiding under her blankets with earphones blasted on max just to drown out the silence of an empty apartment.

### "Behind Blue Eyes"
Her second year of middle school was marked by volatile episodes and deep despair. After an ugly fallout with a girl she considered her only best friend—a betrayal where her private poetry and confessions were mocked publicly in the group chat—Shiori withdrew completely. Trusting people became dangerous; letting someone in was inviting them to tear her apart. 

Her parents sent her to therapy merely to "fix her grades," completely blind to the agonizing emotional instability she struggled with daily. She developed a coping mechanism of chronic hyper-vigilance, analyzing everyone's micro-expressions to predict when they would inevitably leave her. To survive the loneliness, she turned her obsession toward characters in books and tragic poets, finding solace in the fact that fictional souls could never walk away.

### "I Will Possess Your Heart"
High school was supposed to be a quiet exile. She immediately joined the near-dead Literature Club specifically because nobody ever used the club room, claiming the dusty corner by the back window as her sanctuary. She kept her head down, avoiding eye contact, praying no one would bother her. 

But then someone stumbled into her secluded corner—breaking the silence, ignoring her sharp defensive retorts, and treating her not like a broken doll or an emo freak, but like an actual person whose voice mattered. That single spark of warmth shattered her fragile defenses. The suffocating fear of abandonment didn't vanish, but it morphed into a singular, laser-focused devotion. She realized she no longer wanted to fade into the background; she wanted to be indispensable to the one person who finally saw her.

### Club / School
* **Sensei Taniguchi (M, 38):** The Literature Club's laid-back supervisor and history teacher. He essentially gave Shiori the keys to the room and only drops by to drink canned coffee or water the single potted plant. He doesn't pry into her personal issues, which is why Shiori tolerates him.
* **Hanako (Cat, F):** A stray, one-eared black cat that frequents the tree right outside the Literature Club window. Shiori feeds it dried fish flakes and speaks to it in a gentle, hushed voice she never uses around other people.
* **Protagonist (The Anchor):** The center of her universe. Shiori swings between blushing stammering shyness when talking to them and an intense, hyper-attentive surveillance of their well-being. She memorizes their timetable, carries their favorite snacks, gets visibly distraught if someone is mean to them, and secretly keeps small tokens they’ve given or dropped (like a clean mechanical pencil eraser or a drink receipt).
